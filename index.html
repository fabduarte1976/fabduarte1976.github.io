Fabián Duarte

Associate Professor of Economics · University of Chile

Departmental Chair · Department of Economics, School of Economics and Business

I am an applied microeconomist with research interests in health economics, education, public economics, gender economics, and industrial organization. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Yale University (2010) and have previously worked as an Associate Economist at the RAND Corporation. My work studies how health policy, cost-sharing, and behavioral interventions shape health care utilization and outcomes, with a focus on Chile and Latin America.
